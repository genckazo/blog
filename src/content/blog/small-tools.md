People write about the tools that demo well. These are the ones that do not
demo at all.

1. **A plain text file for today's tasks.** Three lines, no sync, no app. It
   has never once failed to load.
2. **grep.** The original search tool. Combined with `git grep` it answers
   "where did I write that?" in half a second.
3. **A good monospace font.** I stare at text for hours; the font is the
   interface.
4. **rsync for backups.** Boring, incremental, and it has saved me twice.
5. **The kitchen timer.** The only productivity tool that also makes lunch.

## The pattern

None of these have a roadmap, a changelog, or a notification badge. They do one
thing, they do it instantly, and they never ask for attention. The best tools
I own are the ones I forget I am using.
