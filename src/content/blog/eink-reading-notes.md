I spent a month reading every evening on an e-ink device instead of a
phone or a tablet with a glowing screen. The results were boring in the best
way: I read more, slept better, and stopped doomscrolling almost entirely.

## What changed

- **My eyes stopped complaining.** By day three, the end-of-evening grittiness
  was gone.
- **I read longer sessions.** Paper-like contrast makes it easy to sink in for
  an hour without noticing.
- **The phone stayed in the other room.** The e-ink device has no apps, no
  notifications, no web. That absence is the feature.

## What I missed

Not much. No color, no video, and page turns that take a little longer than a
scroll. In exchange, the text felt *settled*, like it was printed rather than
projected.

This blog's reading pages borrow the same idea: a warm paper background with
a faint grain, serif type, a comfortable measure, and nothing that glows.

```text
palette:  paper #f3efe3 · ink #2b2820 · one muted accent
type:     serif, 1.7 line height, ~65 characters per line
mood:     lamplight, not laser
```

If you want your own reading to feel calmer, the cheapest change is not a new
device — it is a theme. Turn the lights down on your screen and see how much
nicer the same words look.
